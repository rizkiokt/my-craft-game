How to Play

          

            

              
Getting started

              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    Survival loop
    
              
      
                
    • Punch trees for Wood, then open the inventory to craft Planks and Sticks.
      • 
                
    • Craft a Crafting Table, place it, and look at it to unlock bigger recipes.
      • 
                
    • A Wood Pickaxe mines stone; a Stone Pickaxe mines iron ore.
      • 
                
    • Place a Furnace and look at it to smelt iron ore with coal.
      • 
                
    • Stone and ores need the right tool — the HUD tells you when you need better gear.
      • 
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    Creative mode
    
              
      
                
    • Every block and item is unlocked with unlimited supply.
      • 
                
    • Double-tap Jump to start and stop flying.
      • 
                
    • While flying, Jump rises and Sneak descends.
      • 
                
    • Blocks break instantly and nothing is consumed when you build.
      • 
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    Where to explore
    
              
      
                
    • City district around x 18, z -14 — brick towers, roads, and villagers.
      • 
                
    • Snow realm east near x 112, z 66 — igloos, lodges, and pine forest.
      • 
                
    • Caves and ore veins run below the surface; bring a pickaxe.
